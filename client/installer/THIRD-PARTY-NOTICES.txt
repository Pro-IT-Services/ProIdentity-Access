ProIdentity Access - third-party software
=========================================

ProIdentity Access includes the following third-party software. Each component
is a separate program or driver shipped unmodified alongside ProIdentity Access,
and remains under its own license. Full license texts are in
openvpn\license.txt (Windows) or /Library/ProIdentity/openvpn/COPYING and COPYRIGHT.GPL (macOS).

Copyright (C) 2002-2026 OpenVPN Inc <sales@openvpn.net>
License: GNU General Public License version 2 (GPLv2), with the OpenSSL
